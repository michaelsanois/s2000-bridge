S2000 Bridge

Send sliced programs from your Mac straight into your Akai S2000. No disk images, no floppies.

⬇ Download for Mac (free) ☕ Support the project

AUv2 plugin + standalone app · Beta release · free to use · donations welcome but never required

How it works

Mac→ Raspberry Pi + PiSCSI→ Akai S2000

Slice your audio on the Mac, hit SEND, and S2000 Bridge pushes the program over SCSI directly into the sampler's memory, ready to play.

Features

  • Direct SCSI sendPrograms go straight into sampler RAM rather than through disk images.
  • Slice on your MacChop breaks and loops on a big screen, then send them across.
  • Manage your librariesKeep your sample and program libraries organised on your Mac.
  • Edit S2000 programs on your MacPull programs from the sampler and tweak them on screen instead of the tiny LCD.
  • AU plugin or standalone appRun it as an Audio Unit (AUv2) inside your DAW, or on its own as a standalone app.
  • Automatable parameters via SysExControl S2000 parameters over SysEx and automate them from your DAW.
  • Built-in diagnosticsCheck MIDI and SysEx status and copy a diagnostics report in one click.

You'll need

  • A Mac running macOS
  • Raspberry Pi 3 with a PiSCSI board (tested with RaSCSI v2.3B)
  • An Akai S2000 sampler with a SCSI connection
  • A MIDI interface between the Mac and the S2000
  • Optional: Rosetta OS (S2000 Bridge edition) on the S2000 for recording and onboard FX. Based on Rosetta OS by JunkSmithWizard, MIT licence.
